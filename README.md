# 📊 IBM HR Analytics & Employee Attrition Dashboard

An executive-grade, interactive Excel dashboard built to analyze workforce retention, identify key attrition drivers, and support data-driven HR decision-making.

---

## 🛠️ Tech Stack & Methods
* Data Cleaning & ETL: Power Query
* Data Modeling: Power Pivot (Relational Modeling)
* Calculations: DAX & Custom Calculated Measures
* Visual Interface: Executive Dark Layout (#1E293B) with custom borderless Slicers

---

## 💡 Key Business Insights
* OverTime Impact: 54% of total employee attrition originated from staff working OverTime.
* High-Risk Roles: Sales Representatives recorded the highest churn rate at 39.76%, followed by Laboratory Technicians.
* Travel Frequency: Frequent business travel directly correlated with decreased job satisfaction and elevated turnover.
